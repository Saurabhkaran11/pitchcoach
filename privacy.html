Privacy & Terms

Last updated: 18 September 2026. Plain-language summary — have a lawyer review before charging customers.

What we collect

  • Your pitch text. The transcript (or the text you type) is sent to our language-model provider to be scored and rewritten. We do not store it on our servers.
  • Your voice recording — only if you ask. The recording stays in your browser. It is sent to Boson AI only when you press the 🔊 button and have agreed to voice cloning, so the rewrite can be read in your voice. We do not store the recording or the cloned voice.
  • Your scores and history. Saved in your own browser (localStorage). We cannot see them. "Clear history" deletes them.
  • Your email, if you join the waitlist. Used only to tell you about PitchCoach.
  • Basic, anonymous usage counts (page views) if analytics is enabled. No advertising trackers.

Who processes your data

  • Google (Gemini) — scoring, rewriting, questions.
  • Boson AI (Higgs TTS) — speech and voice cloning.
  • Vercel — hosting and the small server functions that pass requests along.

Each provider handles data under its own terms. Do not pitch confidential information you are not allowed to share.

Voice cloning consent

Only clone your own voice. Do not upload or record someone else. You can withdraw consent any time from the app: the next 🔊 will use a standard voice instead.

Your choices

  • Use typed mode to avoid the microphone entirely.
  • Clear your history in the app, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Ask us to remove your waitlist email: saurabhkaran11@gmail.com.

Terms, briefly

  • PitchCoach gives practice feedback generated by AI. It can be wrong. It is not investment, legal or financial advice, and a high score does not predict funding.
  • Fair use: the free tier is rate-limited. Do not abuse, scrape or resell the service.
  • The service is provided "as is", without warranties.
